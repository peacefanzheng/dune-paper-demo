How it works

Three steps. Eight minutes.

Each book holds thirty paper strips, accordion-folded and infused with essential oils. Tear, light, and let the smolder do the work — no flame, no wax, no wick to trim.

01.
Tear one strip.
Open the book. Pull off one paper accordion strip along the perforated edge. Re-fold so it stands on its own.
— Takes 5 seconds
02.
Light. Then blow out.
Set it on a heatproof dish. Touch a match to the top edge and let it catch. After a few seconds, blow out the flame — it will smolder on its own.
— Smolders, doesn't burn
03.
Eight minutes of scent.
The strip releases warm botanical smoke for about eight minutes, then turns to a clean ash. Empty the dish. Done.
— No wax · No wick · No residue

Our story

We started with one question:
why does a candle take three hours?

Most home scent is built for the wrong moment. A candle wants a long evening. A diffuser hangs around for weeks. We wanted something that lasts as long as a cup of coffee — and disappears when you're done with it.

Dune Paper is folded by hand in Long Beach, California. Each strip is one accordion of textured paper, dipped in a blend of essential oils and saltpeter. Eight minutes of warm botanical smoke. No wax, no wick, no half-burned guilt sitting on your counter.

— I —
Made on the coast
Hand-folded in our Long Beach studio. Small batches. You can tell — the paper is never quite the same twice.
— II —
Eight minute scent
